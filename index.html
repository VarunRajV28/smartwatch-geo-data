Smartwatch Technical Database (Under ₹2,500)

Crossbeats Ignite Spectra

The Crossbeats Ignite Spectra is a square-dial smartwatch optimized for Android. It features a 1.78-inch Super AMOLED display with 650 nits peak brightness. It utilizes a single-chip Bluetooth calling module, which significantly reduces audio latency during voice calls compared to standard dual-chip architectures. The standard retail price is ₹1,999.
